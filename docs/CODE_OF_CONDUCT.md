# Code of Conduct

UQ Reality Labs is committed to a safe, respectful, and productive project environment. This applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, meetings, workshops, demos, online channels, and industry project activities.

## Expected behaviour

Act professionally, respect others, follow lawful and university requirements, and protect private project information. Disagreement is allowed; harassment, intimidation, discrimination, retaliation, and bad-faith disruption are not.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment or discrimination; sexual language or unwelcome attention; intimidation, stalking, doxxing, threats, or sustained disruption; publishing private communications; and disclosing credentials, personal data, paid assets, client material, or confidential project information.

Participants asked to stop unacceptable behaviour must comply immediately.

## AI-assisted work

AI tools may be used where project, course, client, and licence terms allow. The submitter remains responsible for understanding, testing, maintaining, securing, and licensing the work. Do not send private code, credentials, client material, student data, or confidential logs to public AI tools.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to the workshop director, project lead, UQRL organiser, or relevant university channel. Reports should be handled discreetly. Appropriate action may include warnings, removal from project spaces, loss of repository access, referral to university processes, or referral to law enforcement.

Participants must also follow applicable University of Queensland integrity, conduct, privacy, safety, and information-security policies.
